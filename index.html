Who We Are & Our Mandate

Transforming Lives Through Sound Doctrine, Fervent Prayer & Purposeful Leadership

GOFAMINT Pentecost Arena, Ikere-Ekiti stands as a flagship parish of The Gospel Faith Mission International (founded in 1956 by Pastor Dr. R. A. George and led globally by Pastor Dr. Elijah Oludele Abina). Positioned strategically along Ado Road in Afao Quarters, our assembly bridges timeless Pentecostal holiness with contemporary excellence for families, professionals, civil servants, and university students across Ikere-Ekiti.

Under the visionary shepherd-leadership of Pastor Beloved Akinkunmi Abimbola, Pentecost Arena has become renowned not only for vibrant worship and deep expository preaching, but also for pioneering civic and entrepreneurial capacity-building through the Pentecost Leadership Academy (PLA), ministerial equipping via the Pentecost School of Ministry (PSM), and the annual youth awakening conference, EMERGENCE.

Leadership Academies & Campus Fellowship

Beyond Sunday worship, GOFAMINT Pentecost Arena invests heavily in intellectual, civic, ministerial, and student development across Ikere-Ekiti.

Civic & Enterprise Institute

Pentecost Leadership Academy (PLA)

Anchored on the theme "Leading Self to Lead Others," PLA bridges spiritual integrity with practical marketplace, governance, and entrepreneurial competence.

  • Ethical civic leadership & public service keynotes (featuring distinguished leaders such as Rt. Hon. Adeoye Aribasoye).
  • Business structuring, grant procurement workshops & SME mentorship.
  • Open to emerging leaders, professionals, and young entrepreneurs in Ekiti State.
Annual Leadership Cohort
Theological & Ministerial Training

Pentecost School of Ministry (PSM)

Dedicated to raising sound, doctrinally grounded "Torchbearers" equipped for effective ministry in the local church and mission fields.

  • Biblical hermeneutics, expository homiletics, and GOFAMINT foundational doctrines.
  • Pastoral care, discipleship dynamics, and Christian ethics.
  • Culminates in formal graduation and commissioning of ministry workers.
Torchbearers Commissioning
University Student Ministry

GSF Victory Field (BOUESTI)

The vibrant campus arm serving students of Bamidele Olumilua University of Education, Science and Technology, Ikere-Ekiti (BOUESTI).

  • Academic excellence tutorials, exam prayer vigils, and welfare support.

  • Active participation in the annual EMERGENCE youth convocation.
